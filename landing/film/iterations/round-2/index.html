Round 2: motion first

All three cuts tell the same short story: select the headline, give it a new color, leave a note for your agent, send both, and watch the change land in the code. Every frame of the app is a real capture of one continuous session in Design Layer. The film only moves the camera, the pointer, and the light. The cuts are silent; music comes later.

What I took from the references

  • Open on the product doing a real job, and show one clear outcome rather than a tour of features. That's why all three follow a single edit end to end. (Moonb, “13 Software Demo Videos That Sell the Product”)
  • For a developer tool, watching the thing being built beats any animation. So the film never fakes UI; typing reveals the captured text a character at a time. (Same source, on Retool's demo)
  • Linear arrives fast and settles long; Vercel lets silence signal confidence; motion should confirm, not perform. Hence one expo-out curve for arrivals, a smooth in-out for travel, and nothing that moves for its own sake. (DesignMD, “Linear vs Vercel: two approaches to minimal motion”)

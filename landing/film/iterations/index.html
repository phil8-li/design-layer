Three cuts of the launch film

Each cut has its own original soundtrack, and every cut, word, and hit lands on that track's beats. All three use the same Demo app screenshots, and the kinetic type comes from Apple's launch films: one word per beat, big and tight.

A · Bounce B · Cinematic C · Groove

Turn your sound on. The music is written in code (no samples, no licenses), so it can ship with the repo. I can't listen to audio myself, so I checked the mixes with spectrograms and level meters. Please judge the sound by ear.
